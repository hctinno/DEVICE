L106: #### § 2.1091 Radiofrequency radiation exposure evaluation: mobile devices.
L107: 
L108: (a) Requirements of this section are a consequence of Commission responsibilities under the National Environmental Policy Act to evaluate the environmental significance of its actions. See 【22†subpart I of part 1 of this chapter】, in particular 【23†§ 1.1307(b)】.
L109: (b) For purposes of this section, the definitions in 【24†§ 1.1307(b)(2) of this chapter】 shall apply. A mobile device is defined as a transmitting device designed to be used in other than fixed locations and to generally be used in such a way that a separation distance of at least 20 centimeters is normally maintained between the RF source's radiating structure(s) and the body of the user or nearby persons.
L110: In this context, the term “fixed location” means that the device is physically secured at one location and is not able to be easily moved to another location while transmitting. Transmitting devices designed to be used by consumers or workers that can be easily re-located, such as wireless devices associated with a personal desktop computer, are considered to be mobile devices if they meet the 20-centimeter separation requirement.
L111: (c)
L112: (1) Evaluation of compliance with the exposure limits in 【25†§ 1.1310 of this chapter】, and preparation of an EA if the limits are exceeded, is necessary for mobile devices with single RF sources having either more than an available maximum time-averaged power of 1 mW or more than the ERP listed in Table 1 to 【26†§ 1.1307(b)(3)(i)(C)】, whichever is greater.
L113: For mobile devices not exempt by 【26†§ 1.1307(b)(3)(i)(C)】 at distances from 20 centimeters to 40 centimeters and frequencies from 0.3 GHz to 6 GHz, evaluation of compliance with the exposure limits in 【25†§ 1.1310 of this chapter】 is necessary if the ERP of the device is greater than ERP_{20cm} in the formula below.
L114: If the ERP of a single RF source at distances from 20 centimeters to 40 centimeters and frequencies from 0.3 GHz to 6 GHz is not easily obtained, then the available maximum time-averaged power may be used (i.e., without consideration of ERP) in comparison with the following formula only if the physical dimensions of the radiating structure(s) do not exceed the electrical length of λ/4 or if the antenna gain is less than that of a half-wave dipole (1.64 linear value).
