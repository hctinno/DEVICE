L107: #### § 2.938 Retention of records.
L108: ###### Cross Reference
L109: 
L110: Link to an amendment published at 【22†90 FR 38067†www.federalregister.gov】, Aug. 7, 2025.
L111: 
L112: (a) For equipment subject to the equipment authorization procedures in this part, the responsible party shall maintain the records listed as follows:
L113: 
L114: (1) A record of the original design drawings and specifications and all changes that have been made that may affect compliance with the standards and the requirements of 【23†§ 2.931】.
L115: (2) A record of the procedures used for production inspection and testing to ensure conformance with the standards and the requirements of 【23†§ 2.931】.
L116: 
L117: (3) A record of the test results that demonstrate compliance with the appropriate regulations in this chapter.
L118: 
L119: (b) For equipment subject to Supplier's Declaration of Conformity, the responsible party shall, in addition to the requirements in 【24†paragraph (a)】 of this section, maintain the following records:
L120: (1) Measurements made on an appropriate test site that demonstrates compliance with the applicable regulations in this chapter. The record shall:
L121: 
L122: (i) Indicate the actual date all testing was performed;
L123: 
L124: (ii) State the name of the test laboratory, company, or individual performing the testing;
L125: 
L126: (iii) Contain a description of how the device was actually tested, identifying the measurement procedure and test equipment that was used;
L127: (iv) Contain a description of the equipment under test (EUT) and support equipment connected to, or installed within, the EUT;
L128: 
L129: (v) Identify the EUT and support equipment by trade name and model number and, if appropriate, by FCC Identifier and serial number;
L130: 
L131: (vi) Indicate the types and lengths of connecting cables used and how they were arranged or moved during testing;
L132: (vii) Contain at least two drawings or photographs showing the test set-up for the highest line conducted emission and showing the test set-up for the highest radiated emission. These drawings or photographs must show enough detail to confirm other information contained in the test report. Any photographs used must clearly show the test configuration used;
