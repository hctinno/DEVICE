L106: #### § 2.1093 Radiofrequency radiation exposure evaluation: portable devices.
L107: 
L108: (a) Requirements of this section are a consequence of Commission responsibilities under the National Environmental Policy Act to evaluate the environmental significance of its actions. See 【22†subpart I of part 1 of this chapter】, in particular 【23†§ 1.1307(b)】.
L109: (b) For purposes of this section, the definitions in 【24†§ 1.1307(b)(2) of this chapter】 shall apply. A portable device is defined as a transmitting device designed to be used in other than fixed locations and to generally be used in such a way that the RF source's radiating structure(s) is/are within 20 centimeters of the body of the user.
L110: 
L111: (c)
L112: (1) Evaluation of compliance with the exposure limits in 【25†§ 1.1310 of this chapter】, and preparation of an EA if the limits are exceeded, is necessary for portable devices having single RF sources with more than an available maximum time-averaged power of 1 mW, more than the ERP listed in Table 1 to 【26†§ 1.1307(b)(3)(i)(C)】, or more than the P_{th} in the following formula, whichever is greater.
L113: The following formula shall only be used in conjunction with portable devices not exempt by 【26†§ 1.1307(b)(3)(i)(C)】 at distances from 0.5 centimeters to 20 centimeters and frequencies from 0.3 GHz to 6 GHz.
L114: d = the minimum separation distance (cm) in any direction from any part of the device antenna(s) or radiating structure(s) to the body of the device user.
L115: 
L116: (2) For multiple mobile or portable RF sources within a device operating in the same time averaging period, evaluation is required if the formula in 【27†§ 1.1307(b)(3)(ii)(B) of this chapter】 is applied to determine the exemption ratio and the result is greater than 1.
L117: (3) Unless otherwise specified in this chapter, any other single portable or multiple mobile and portable RF source(s) associated with a device is exempt from routine environmental evaluation for RF exposure prior to equipment authorization or use, except as specified in 【28†§ 1.1307(c)】 and 【29†(d) of this chapter】.
