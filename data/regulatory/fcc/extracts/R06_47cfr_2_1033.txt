L107: #### § 2.1033 Application for certification.
L108: ###### Cross Reference
L109: 
L110: Link to an amendment published at 【22†90 FR 38070†www.federalregister.gov】, Aug. 7, 2025.
L111: 
L112: (a) An application for certification shall be filed on FCC Form 731 with all questions answered. Items that do not apply shall be so noted.
L113: 
L114: (b) Applications for equipment operating under Parts 11, 15 and 18 of the rules shall be accompanied by a technical report containing the following information:
L115: (1) The full name, mailing address and physical address (if different from mailing address), email address, and telephone number of:
L116: 
L117: (i) The applicant for certification; and
L118: 
L119: (ii) The applicant's agent for service of process in the United States for matters relating to the authorized equipment.
L120: 
L121: (2) A written and signed certification that, as of, the filing date of the notice, the equipment to which the change applies is not prohibited from receiving an equipment authorization pursuant to 【23†§ 2.903】;
L122: (3) An affirmative or negative statement as to whether the applicant is identified on the Covered List, established pursuant to 【24†§ 1.50002 of this chapter】, as an entity producing covered communications equipment; and
L123: 
L124: (4) The written and signed certifications required by 【25†§ 2.911(d)(7)】.
L125: 
L126: (5) FCC identifier.
L127: (6) A copy of the installation and operating instructions to be furnished the user. A draft copy of the instructions may be submitted if the actual document is not available. The actual document shall be furnished to the FCC when it becomes available.
L128: 
L129: (7) A brief description of the circuit functions of the device along with a statement describing how the device operates. This statement should contain a description of the ground system and antenna, if any, used with the device.
L130: (8) A block diagram showing the frequency of all oscillators in the device. The signal path and frequency shall be indicated at each block. The tuning range(s) and intermediate frequency(ies) shall be indicated at each block. A schematic diagram is also required for intentional radiators.
